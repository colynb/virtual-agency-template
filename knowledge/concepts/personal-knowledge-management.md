# Personal Knowledge Management

## Summary

Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) is the practice of collecting, organizing, and retrieving information for individual use. The goal is to build a personal repository of knowledge that grows more valuable over time.

## Detail

PKM systems vary from simple folder-based note collections to sophisticated interlinked knowledge bases. Modern approaches emphasize:

- **Capture** — Get ideas out of your head quickly (inbox/quick capture)
- **Organize** — Categorize, tag, and link notes so they're findable later
- **Connect** — The highest-value step: linking ideas reveals patterns and insights
- **Retrieve** — Search, browse, and traverse your knowledge when you need it
- **Review** — Periodically revisit and refine your knowledge base

Popular tools: Obsidian, Notion, Roam Research, Logseq, Zettelkasten method (analog or digital).

The agency's own `inbox/ → knowledge/` pipeline implements a PKM workflow where capture is manual but organization is agent-assisted.

## Connections

PKM relies on **knowledge graphs** as the underlying data model for connecting ideas, and **concept mapping** as a way to visualize those connections. This agency itself is a PKM system, with Soren handling the organize/connect steps.
